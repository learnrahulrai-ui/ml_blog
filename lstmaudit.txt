## AUDIT 4: Axiom rule on "word", "movie review", "liked (1) or not (0)" -- MISFIRES

These are the problem statement, not imported technical terms. A geometry book
can say "a point" without deriving what a point is from particles. Setup
context is not the same as introduced terminology. The rule targets: do not
name a TECHNICAL DEVICE before its mechanical need is shown. "Movie review"
is not a technical device.

The flags that do matter from this list:
  - "grid" -- named before the assembly of multiple clerks is made explicit.
    That is a real gap: show a row of clerks side by side, THEN call the
    result a grid.
  - "vault" -- metaphor imported, not derived. Could replace with "store" or
    "long pocket" consistently.
  - "votes" -- democratic metaphor; not fatal but not derived either.
  - "dimmer knob" -- electrical metaphor; same.

## RECONSTRUCTION ORDER AT THE BOTTOM -- LARGELY GOOD

The 9-step sequence the audit proposes is actually the right skeleton:
  1. clerk from scratch
  2. row-width from simultaneous-duty constraint
  3. carried pocket from commutativity proof
  4. grid from multiple clerks
  5. tanh from blow-up necessity
  6. sigmoid from 0..1 need
  7. two-pocket from single-pocket fading proof
  8. four machines from enumeration of needed quantities
  9. combine from wiring

This is closer to the current structure than the audit implies. Steps 1, 3, 4,
5, 7, 9 are already present. Steps 2, 6, 8 are the real gaps.

Step 6 is the biggest gap: sigmoid is currently named and its numbers given,
but the move from "need a 0..1 number" to "use this particular S-curve" is
not closed. One sentence can close it: sigmoid(x) = 1/(1+e^-x) maps any real
number to 0..1 and is smooth -- pick it.

Step 8 is also a gap: the current post introduces four machines by naming them
(fresh, keep, admit, show). It does not first ask "what quantities does the
combine NEED?" and enumerate the answer. That enumeration would justify exactly
four, not three or five.

## THE FATAL PROBLEM: AUDIT 2 CONTRADICTS AUDIT 1

  Audit 1 said: tanh, sigmoid, "theorem" are TOO ABSTRACT / too mathematical;
    they need PHYSICAL grounding, no imported math authority.
  Audit 2 says: DERIVE tanh from e via power series and limit proofs; prove
    the fade with the chain rule and matrix norms; define inputs as orthogonal
    basis vectors in R^V.

  These cannot both be satisfied. One demands less math abstraction; the other
  demands a great deal more. Picking Audit 2 throws out Audit 1 entirely.
  Whichever auditor persona is run next will likely contradict this one too.
  A standard must be chosen ONCE, not re-derived per audit.


## IT VIOLATES THE BLOG'S OWN CHARTER

  This blog's stated method, on every page: ban the jargon, draw it first,
  solve by PENCIL, code last. The Charter counts cost in clerk-steps and keeps
  every machine out of the teaching path.

  Deriving e via (1 + 1/n)^n, proving lim tanh = 1 from the exponential
  definition, and bounding ||W|| < 1 to show product-convergence is exactly
  the heavy machinery the Charter bans. It is LESS from-scratch for this
  audience, not more: working 1.5^100 by hand (current text) is more
  elementary and more honest "from scratch" than invoking a power series for
  e. Audit 2 mistakes "more formal" for "more fundamental." They are not the
  same. A reader who needs the post does not have the power series for e.


## A TECHNICAL ERROR IN AUDIT 2

  It conflates two different decays:
    - FORWARD activation decay: the carried signal shrinks across 90 squashes.
      This is what the current post proves, with arithmetic (0.2 * 0.5^k).
    - BACKWARD gradient vanishing: the chain-rule product of Jacobians shrinks.
      This is what Audit 2's "chain rule, ||W^T|| < 1" actually describes.
  Both are real and related, but they are NOT the same proof. The post chose
  the forward, arithmetic version on purpose -- it needs no backprop, which the
  post never builds. Audit 2 demands a gradient proof for a post that has no
  gradients in it. That is a scope explosion, not a fix.


## THE FEW REAL GRAINS

     word" can be grounded by a selector vector [0 0 1 0 ...] picking one row

     selector picks a row. Do NOT phrase it as "orthogonal basis vectors
     isolating semantic variances" -- that smuggles in three undefined
     abstractions ("orthogonal basis", "semantic variance", "projection")

  2. THE CONSTANT ERROR CAROUSEL is the GENUINE reason for the cell state.

     who already have backprop -- NOT inlined into a pencil-arithmetic piece

  3. RESTATE-VARIABLES and ASCII-FIRST: already covered under Audit 1; no new
